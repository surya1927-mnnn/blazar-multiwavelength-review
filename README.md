# Multiwavelength Observations, Spectral Energy Distributions, and Variability of Blazars

## Overview

This repository contains my academic literature review on blazars, focusing on their multiwavelength observational properties, spectral energy distributions (SEDs), variability, emission mechanisms, and multi-messenger connections.

The review discusses observations across the electromagnetic spectrum, from radio and infrared wavelengths to X-rays and gamma rays, and examines the physical processes associated with relativistic jets in blazars.

## Topics Covered

- Multiwavelength observations of blazars
- Radio, infrared, optical, and ultraviolet emission
- X-ray and gamma-ray observations
- Blazar spectral energy distributions (SEDs)
- Synchrotron radiation
- Inverse Compton scattering
- Synchrotron Self-Compton (SSC) emission
- External Compton (EC) emission
- Hadronic emission models
- Short-term and long-term variability
- Multi-messenger astronomy
- Neutrino connections
- Current challenges in blazar research
- Future observational prospects

## Figures

## Purpose

This review was prepared to develop a deeper understanding of high-energy astrophysical phenomena and the observational and theoretical methods used to study relativistic jets in blazars.
